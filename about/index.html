About Number 8 AI

Kiwis have always been good at making do with number 8 wire. Number 8 AI is about doing the same with AI: finding the practical tools that save a small business real hours, without the hype.

Why we built it

There are thousands of AI tools out there and most lists are written for Silicon Valley start-ups. We wanted one for the sparky in Hamilton, the café in Dunedin and the online store run from a spare room in Tauranga — what the tool does, roughly what it costs, and whether it works with the software NZ businesses already use, like Xero.

How we choose tools

  • Every tool is a real product with a working website, checked on 2026-10-15.
  • Pricing notes come from the provider's own site. If we couldn't confirm it, we say “See site”.
  • “NZ-made” and other NZ notes are only added where we could verify them.
  • We highlight NZ-made tools wherever good ones exist.
  • Sponsored placements are always clearly labelled and never change editorial listings.
